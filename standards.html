Certification Bodies

Independent, accredited laboratories and institutes that test and certify security products.

Our certifications are issued by accredited, independent European and international testing laboratories. Each standard defines precise attack scenarios, tools, and time thresholds — so when a Luxuria safe carries a grade, it is a guarantee backed by science, not marketing.

European Certification Board

ECB·S

The European Certification Board for Security Systems (ECB·S) is one of Europe's most respected independent certification bodies. ECB·S certifies products across the full EN 1143-1 grade range and is recognised by major insurance underwriters throughout the continent.

Underwriters Laboratories

UL

Underwriters Laboratories As a US-based institute, Underwriters Laboratories is one of the world's leading independent safety organizations. UL certifications verify that products meet strict standards for security, durability and fire resistance through rigorous laboratory testing. UL 687 UL 72 Class 350

The Research Institutes of Sweden

RI.SE

RI.SE is Sweden’s independent research and certification institute. With any safe, vault or cabinet adorned with the RI.SE recognised mark, you can be assured that the product will operate to the highest level, ensuring maximum protection for the long haul.

Bureau of Indian Standards

BIS

Bureau of Indian Standards BIS is India's National Standard Body, renowned for its quality and commitment to rigorous product testing and development. Their mission is to deliver safe, highly reliable products that keep personal, corporate, and high-risk business assets completely secure. IS 11188 (Part 1) IS 11188 (Part 2) IS 14512

South African Bureau of Standards

SABS

SABS is the South African Bureau of Standards certification. As an organisation, they focus heavily on product performance, safety and longevity, focusing on robust testing procedures in line with national regulations. Trusted, reliable, and independent.

French Certification / CNPP

A2P - Assurance Prévention Protection

Issued by the CNPP (National Centre for Prevention and Protection), A2P certification classifies safes into three star levels based on resistance to burglary attempts. It is mandatory for insurance-recognised security in France and widely adopted across French-speaking markets.

Burglary Resistance

Laboratory-tested resistance against physical attack, tools, and forced entry.

EN
1143-1
ECB-S

EN 1143-1 — Secure Storage Units

Europe's leading certification for high-security safes, vault doors and strongrooms. Products are tested against professional burglary tools and assigned a security grade based on the level of protection achieved. Security grades are determined using internationally standardized testing methods carried out by independent laboratories.

Available Grades Safes Grade 0-VI Vault Doors Grade 0-XIII Modular Vault Rooms Grade 0-XIII
EN
14450
ECB-S

EN 14450 — Secure Storage Cabinets

Designed primarily for domestic environments or lower-risk commercial settings, this standard tests light security cabinets against forced entries using lighter hand tools. It bridges the gap between basic, unrated lockboxes and heavy-duty graded safes.

Available Classes Level S1 Level S2

UL 687 certifies burglary-resistant safes and vault doors by testing how well they withstand professional attack using a range of specialized tools.

Fire Resistance

Proven protection for documents, media, and valuables against extreme heat.

EN
1047-1
European Standard

EN 1047-1 — Secure Storage Units (Fire)

EN 1047-1 is the premier European standard for fire-resistant document cabinets and safes. Products are exposed to external furnace temperatures of up to 1,090 °C while the internal temperature must remain below 170 °C for paper and 52 °C for digital media. An impact drop and explosion test are also performed.

Our fire-rated range is certified to EN 1047-1 Class S60P and S120P.
Protection Classes S 60 P S 120 P S 60 DIS S 120 DIS
EN
15659
European Standard

EN 15659 — Light Fire Protection

EN 15659 covers lighter-weight fire protection products for paper documents. Products are tested at furnace temperatures up to 1,049 °C with a post-fire cool-down period. This standard is particularly suited to office environments requiring accessible, everyday fire protection for important documents.

Entry-level Luxuria fire safes achieve LFS 30P and LFS 60P classification.
Fire Classes LFS 30 P LFS 60 P
NT
FIRE
Nordic / International

NT Fire — Nordic Fire Testing

The NT Fire series (017, 032, etc.) are internationally recognised fire-resistance test methods published by Nordtest, widely accepted in Europe, North America, and Asia. NT Fire 017 is especially common for paper-rated safes, while NT Fire 032 covers magnetic and digital media protection.

Our digital media safes carry NT Fire 017 — 60 minutes and NT Fire 032 approvals.
Test Methods NT Fire 017 NT Fire 032
UL 72
Underwriters Laboratories

UL 72 - Fire Resistance of Record Protection Equipment

The foundational American thermal benchmark certifying safe interiors against high external temperatures while continuously monitoring relative humidity metrics inside the safe. Class 350 corresponds to tested and certified to conditions where the interior temperature remains below 350°F / 177°C for Paper Documents and rated for 1-hour or 2-hour variants.

Available Core Classifications Class 350

EXPLOSIVES & ADVANCED T2 ATTACKS

Protection against advanced attacks.

EN
1143-1 EX
ECB-S

EN 1143-1 EX — Secure Storage Units (Explosives)

An elite sub-classification added to the baseline EN 1143-1 burglary grade. Units must withstand a live blast attack using high explosives without compromising the locking mechanism or creating an interior breach.

Available Classes Grade I EX to Grade VI EX Grade I EX to Grade XIII EX for Vaults
T2
ECB-S

T2 - Advanced Tooling Resistance Standard

An upgraded certification tier under EN 1143-1 applied to modern high-security safes. This standard updates baseline testing to include defense against newly developed, highly destructive tools frequently deployed by modern sophisticated crime syndicates.

Available T2 Statuses Validated across high-tier Graded Safes, ATM modules, and Strongrooms.

Lock Standards

Every locking mechanism certified to European high-security lock standards.

EN
1300
European Standard

EN 1300 — High-Security Locks

EN 1300 is the definitive European standard for evaluating and classifying high-security locks for safes and strongrooms. Locks are tested for manipulation, covert attack, and forced entry, and classified into four grades — A through D — with D representing the highest resistance.

All Luxuria lock mechanisms are certified to EN 1300 Grade B and Grade C.
Security Grades Grade A Grade B Grade C Grade D
A2P
SERRURE
French Certification / CNPP

A2P — Certified Safe Locks

Complementing the A2P safe certification, the A2P lock certificate specifically validates the locking mechanism in isolation. Tested by CNPP laboratories against manipulation and forced attack, A2P-certified locks are a prerequisite for any safe seeking the three-star A2P product mark.

All Luxuria locks are individually A2P certified prior to integration.
Certification A2P Certified
