Watch Winding Technology

Chronovision — The Pinnacle of Watch Winding

Experience the epitome of watch winding technology with Chronovision — a marvel of sophistication in watch care. Through seamless control of modules via a Bluetooth app, customers embark on an extraordinary journey. The system boasts individually adjustable motors and up to 64 controllable modules, presenting an unparalleled level of customisation. Crafted with precision, Chronovision technology ensures your watches are cared for with the utmost attention to detail and reliability.

German Precision

Heisse & Söhne — A Masterpiece of Precision

Indulge in precision and luxury with the Heisse & Söhne Basic Watch Winder. With up to 24 individually adjustable motors and a smooth rotating drum adorned with multiple bearings, your watches are kept in perfect condition. The telescopic clock clasp offers seamless adjustments, assuring a secure fit for your prized possessions. Available as a Starter Kit of four modules, expandable up to 24.
