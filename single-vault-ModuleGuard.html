Security Vaults

ModuleGuard Strongroom

The ModuleGuard Strongroom System is a modular, high-security enclosure designed for modern commercial environments requiring certified protection and installation flexibility. Its lightweight, panel-based design allows it to be installed in existing buildings, including upper floors with structural load limitations.

Burglary
Fire

Key Features

  • Installation Flexibility
    Lightweight, bolt-together modular panels require no on-site welding and are ideal for retrofit projects in restricted-access buildings.
  • Tested Attack Resistance
    Certified to EN 1143-1 against hand tools, heavy mechanical tools, and thermal cutting equipment.
  • Sustainability-Focused
    Developed with reduced material weight and optimized manufacturing to minimize environmental impact.

Construction

  • Rapid Deployment
    Modular architecture enables fast installation compared to traditional reinforced concrete vaults.
  • Comprehensive Secure Envelope
    Typically includes wall, ceiling, and floor panels integrated with certified strongroom doors.
  • Expandable & Relocatable
    The scalable structure allows for future expansion or complete relocation of the system rather than demolition.

Applications

  • Bank Strongrooms and Cash Handling Facilities
    Perfect for cash centers and facilities that require secure access control and fire protection.
  • Jewellery and Luxury Retail Vault Rooms
    Designed for high-security storage of valuable items in retail environments.
  • Government, Defence and Pharmaceutical Facilities
    Providing secure storage solutions for sensitive documents and valuables in various settings.
