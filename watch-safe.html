Winding Technology

Chronovision — The Pinnacle of Watch Winding

Experience the pinnacle of watch winding technology with Chronovision. Through the seamless control of modules via a Bluetooth app, customers embark on a truly extraordinary journey. The system boasts up to 64 individually controllable motors, presenting an unparalleled level of customisation. Crafted with precision and pride, our watch winders exclusively employ premium components fully made in Germany for superior performance and reliability.

  • Individually adjustable motors, tailored to your watch's unique needs
  • App control with an integrated watch database of ~5,000 movement settings
  • Gearbox protection through slip control
  • 12-hour stop position during rest periods
  • Freely selectable rotation: left, right, or alternating
  • Rotations per day adjustable from 500 to 2,650 in increments of 50
  • Intelligent programmable sleep phase up to 14 hours
  • Patented synchronous running for harmonised winding
  • 3 years warranty

German Engineering

Heisse & Söhne — The Height of Precision

Immerse yourself in the world of precision and luxury with the Heisse & Söhne Basic Watch Winder. With the ability to connect, program, and effortlessly control up to 24 motors via a single control unit with LCD and touch display, you can indulge your cherished timepieces in a truly exceptional experience. The rotating drum adorned with multiple bearings delivers smooth, gentle rotation, while the telescopic clock clasp accommodates various bracelet sizes.

  • Individually adjustable motors
  • 12-hour stop position in rest periods
  • Freely selectable rotation direction: left, right, alternating
  • Rotations per day: 450 / 550 / 650 / 750 / 850 / 950 / 1,200 / 1,500
  • Switchable sleep phase (22:00–11:00)
  • Integrated illumination
  • Expandable up to 24 modules
