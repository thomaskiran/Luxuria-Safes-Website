Centurion Strongroom

The Centurion Strongroom is an elite security system engineered to protect bank strongrooms, vault rooms, and high-value storage areas. Designed for environments requiring extreme resistance against both burglary and intense physical attack, it serves as the core component of certified secure enclosures.

Burglary
Fire

Key Features

  • Scalable Elite Security
    Available in a wide range of security grades from Grade V up to Grade XIII to match specific risk levels.
  • Extreme Attack Resistance
    Engineered with multi-layer security structures and high-density composite barrier materials to absorb and resist forced entry.
  • Versatile Configuration
    Offered in multiple variants including standard single-leaf, lightweight, round door, and door-within-door emergency access options.

Construction

  • Intelligent Locking Integration
    Supports dual-lock configurations, time-delay functions, and audit trail logging for electronic systems.
  • Seamless System Integration
    Designed to operate as part of a complete security envelope, integrating with strongroom wall panels, ceiling/floor reinforcement, and alarm systems.
  • Professional Finish
    Standard protective industrial coating with optional RAL color finishing for architectural integration.

Applications

  • Bank Strongrooms and Cash Handling Facilities
    Perfect for cash centers and facilities that require secure access control and fire protection.
  • Jewellery and Luxury Retail Vault Rooms
    Designed for high-security storage of valuable items in retail environments.
  • Government, Defence and Pharmaceutical Facilities
    Providing secure storage solutions for sensitive documents and valuables in various settings.
