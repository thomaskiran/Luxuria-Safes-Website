Security Vault Doors

Chubb 3.5" Strongroom Door

A higher-security solution engineered for environments requiring increased resistance against modern attack methods. It offers enhanced barrier thickness, stronger boltwork, and upgraded locking protection.

Key Features

  • Modern Tool Resistance
    Designed specifically to resist high-speed drilling, mechanical force, and modern cutting tools.
  • Heavy-Duty Locking
    Upgraded 11-lever double-bitted key lock for higher manipulation resistance.
  • Redundant Safety
    Dual glass relockers provide enhanced anti-tamper protection.

Construction

  • Enhanced Barrier
    Increased protective barrier thickness of 89 mm for superior physical defense.
  • Superior Bolt Strength
    Features larger 50 mm diameter bolts for massive resistance.
  • Reinforced Build
    Constructed with F80 barrier material plus steel fibers for increased density.

Applications

  • High Value Commercial Vaults
    Ideal for high-value retail, and commercial environments requiring secure storage solutions.
  • Bank & Financial Institutions
    Designed for the secure storage of cash, checks, and other high-value items in banking and financial environments.
  • Jewelry Stores
    Specifically engineered for the secure storage of high-value jewelry and accessories.
