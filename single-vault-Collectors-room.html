Collector's Room

The Collector’s Room is a bespoke private strongroom concept that transforms high-security storage into a refined architectural space. It combines the structural security of Gunnebo modular systems with luxury interior design to safeguard jewelry, watches, firearms, and private assets.

Burglary
Fire

Key Features

  • Luxury Aesthetic
    Designed as a private luxury gallery rather than a standard vault, featuring premium wood veneer, leather panels, and lacquer surfaces.
  • Discreet Security
    Capable of being installed as hidden or visible safe configurations within villas, penthouses, or basements.
  • Fully Bespoke Design
    Custom room dimensions, lighting, and storage configurations tailored to the specific needs of the collector.

Construction

  • Complete Infrastructure
    Integrates security with HVAC climate control for temperature and humidity stability to protect sensitive valuables.
  • Advanced Monitoring
    Designed to operate as part of a complete security envelope, integrating with strongroom wall panels, ceiling/floor reinforcement, and alarm systems.
  • Certified Foundation
    Built upon a structural core of EN 1143-1 certified modular systems (ModuleGuard or Centurion) for graded burglary resistance.

Applications

  • High Net-Worth Residential Safe Rooms
    Engineered for the ultimate protection of high-value items in exclusive residential settings.
  • Private Jewellery and Luxury Watch Display Rooms
    Designed for the secure presentation and storage of high-value jewellery and luxury timepieces.
  • Armored Storage for Firearms and Private Bullion
    Specifically engineered for the secure storage of firearms and private bullion.
