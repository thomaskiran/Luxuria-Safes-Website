Lock Standards

Every locking mechanism certified to European high-security lock standards.

Every Luxuria Safe is built around one fundamental principle: uncompromising security. While the strength of a safe begins with its construction, its true integrity lies in the quality of its locking system. For this reason, Luxuria Safes are equipped exclusively with internationally certified locking systems that have been independently tested and approved by the world’s leading certification bodies.

European Standard

EN 1300 — High-Security Locks

EN 1300 is the definitive European standard for evaluating and classifying high-security locks for safes and strongrooms. Locks are tested for manipulation, covert attack, and forced entry, and classified into four grades — A through D — with D representing the highest resistance.

All Luxuria lock mechanisms are certified to EN 1300 Grade B and Grade C.
Security Grades Grade A Grade B Grade C Grade D
French Certification / CNPP

A2P — Certified Safe Locks

Complementing the A2P safe certification, the A2P lock certificate specifically validates the locking mechanism in isolation. Tested by CNPP laboratories against manipulation and forced attack, A2P-certified locks are a prerequisite for any safe seeking the three-star A2P product mark.

All Luxuria locks are individually A2P certified prior to integration.
Certification A2P Certified
Underwriters Laboratories

UL

The definitive standard for high-security physical and electronic hardware in North America. UL evaluates locks against targeted physical attack, electromagnetic tampering, environmental stress, and component longevity. Our systems are certified under strict UL Group 2 standards for mechanical dial systems and UL Type 1 High-Security Electronic listings for digital interfaces.

All Luxuria locks are individually UL Certified prior to integration.
Certification UL Group 2 UL Type 1 High-Security
VdS Schadenverhütung GmbH

VdS — Certified Safe Locks

As Europe’s most rigorous independent testing institution for fire and burglary protection, VdS assesses structural security, physical endurance, and mechanical manipulation resistance. Locks across our portfolio carry prestigious VdS Class 1, Class 2, and Class 3 certifications, proving their integrity against specialized drilling and decryption tools.

All Luxuria lock mechanisms are certified to VdS Class 1 and Class 2.
Security Grades Class 1 Class 2 Class 3

Wittkopp OPTIMA 2648 Key Lock (Grade A / VdS Class 1)

Description:

The OPTIMA 2648 is a masterpiece of German physical engineering designed specifically for mounting directly onto high-security safe boltworks. Relying on robust, low-wear mechanical components, it stands as a classic VdS Class 1 / EN 1300 Class A high-security safe lock. It employs a heavy-duty, die-cast zinc block bolt driven by a precise 8-lever system. A uniquely formatted symmetrical double-bitted key interfaces with the internal mechanism, yielding over 800,000 theoretical key code variations to surpass rigorous anti-picking criteria.

Security Feature:

  • Integrated Tamper Protection: Engineered with built-in scanning and mechanical breakdown protection to foil professional picking, manipulation, and physical drilling attempts.
  • Sealed Armor Profile: Features a fully screwed, tamper-resistant lock cover that secures internal levers from unauthorized external observation or casting manipulation.
  • True Mechanical Reliability: Purely mechanical architecture that remains immune to all electronic tampering, signal bypass, or environmental power interruptions.

Sargent & Greenleaf (S&G) Titan Lock (Grade B / VdS Class 2)

Description:

The Titan™ Series represents a rugged, high-durability electronic safe lock designed to withstand heavy, continuous use in residential and demanding commercial applications. Built around a motor-driven locking mechanism with a heavy-duty motorized blocking device, the Titan withstands over 225 lbs. of aggressive end and side bolt pressure. It features an advanced, exclusive no-twist cable design running through a protective channel in the spindle to prevent pinching or wire damage over years of keypad rotation. Engineered to meet the highest security standards, it has been independently tested and proven resistant to military-grade EMP (Electromagnetic Pulse) attacks following strict military standards (MIL-STD461F).

Security Feature:

  • 10-User Code Capacity: Manages 1 Master, 1 Supervisor, 1 Time Delay Override, and up to 7 or 8 independent User Codes.
  • Three Selectable Operating Modes: Configurable via keypad to Single Control, Dual Control (requiring two separate codes within one minute to open), or Manager/Employee mode.
  • Holdup & Tamper Defense: Integrated 1 to 99-minute programmable time delay (with 1 to 15-minute open windows) and a 10-minute penalty lockout if 5 consecutive wrong codes are entered.
  • External Battery Maintenance: Features a low-profile, sure-grip keypad housing an efficient 9V battery compartment accessible entirely from the outside.

Gunnebo Encry Electronic Lock (Grade C / VdS Class 3)

Description:

The Gunnebo Encry™ is an enterprise-tier, ultra-high-security electronic locking ecosystem engineered for heavy-duty safes, commercial strongrooms, and vault doors. Acting as a vanguard against modern electronic attacks, the Encry relies on a smart push-pull motor-driven deadbolt and features fully authenticated hardware and software modules. Utilizing specialized cryptographic keys, it employs advanced communication encryption between its terminal points. In a testament to its zero-trust design, if any hardware component is physically intercepted or falsified, all internal encryption keys are instantly wiped from the system's memory to completely lock down the asset.

Security Feature:

  • One-Time-Code (OTC) Algorithm: Supports dynamic, synchronized OTC generation alongside traditional fixed codes, heavily utilized in high-risk Cash-in-Transit (CIT) and commercial banking networks.
  • Scalable Network Architecture: Can operate as a standalone secure unit or scale into an integrated network connecting up to 15 secure locks managed centrally via SafeControl Manager software.
  • Massive User & Schedule Matrix: Manages up to 50 distinct user codes with customizable profiles, featuring 4 separate time-delay windows (1–99 minutes), 4 opening windows, and up to 30 fully programmable calendar blocking days.
  • Hardened Audit Trail: Automatically logs up to 2,000 secure, non-volatile event logs stamped with exact real-time dates and times for absolute audit accountability.

SecuRam ScanLogic Basic Biometric Lock (Grade C)

Description:

The ScanLogic Basic combines advanced biometric access with simplicity and reliability. It supports up to 3 six-digit access codes, each accommodating up to 5 stored fingerprints—for a total of 15. The RF sensor reads fingerprints in approximately one second, ensuring fast, secure entry. After successful authentication, the user must manually operate the safe handle to open. Powered by a 9-volt battery, the lock offers around 4,000 openings. Its standard mounting footprint ensures compatibility with most safe installations.

Security Feature:

  • Stores up to 15 unique fingerprints and 3 different User Codes (1 Management Code and 2 User Codes).
  • Runs on a single 9-volt alkaline battery, providing over 4,000+ openings.
  • The battery compartment drops down directly below the keypad for quick and easy replacement.
