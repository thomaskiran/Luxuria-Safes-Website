Our Locks

Every Luxuria Safe is built around one fundamental principle: uncompromising security. While the strength of a safe begins with its construction, its true integrity lies in the quality of its locking system. For this reason, Luxuria Safes are equipped exclusively with internationally certified locking systems that have been independently tested and approved by the world’s leading certification bodies.

A2P-Certified Locks

All our safe locks are certified A2P in accordance with the European standard EN 1300, a standard dedicated to high-security locks for safes. EN 1300 classifies locks into 4 levels of security against break-ins: A, B, C, and D (D being the highest level of resistance to break-ins). The classification is based on the observed resistance following intrusion tests conducted by the laboratory of the National Center for Prevention and Protection (CNPP).

It supports two users, features a motorized lock-blocking mechanism capable of withstanding 225 pounds of pressure, and comes in PivotBolt (automatically relocking) or Direct Drive (manually locking) models.

SecuRam ScanLogic Basic Biometric Lock (Grade C)

Description:

The ScanLogic Basic combines advanced biometric access with simplicity and reliability. It supports up to 3 six-digit access codes, each accommodating up to 5 stored fingerprints—for a total of 15. The RF sensor reads fingerprints in approximately one second, ensuring fast, secure entry. After successful authentication, the user must manually operate the safe handle to open. Powered by a 9-volt battery, the lock offers around 4,000 openings. Its standard mounting footprint ensures compatibility with most safe installations.

Security Feature:

  • Stores up to 15 unique fingerprints and 3 different User Codes (1 Management Code and 2 User Codes).
  • Runs on a single 9-volt alkaline battery, providing over 4,000+ openings.
  • The battery compartment drops down directly below the keypad for quick and easy replacement.

SecuRam SafeLogic Basic Direct-Drive Locks

Description:

Ideal for safes with direct-drive boltwork, the SafeLogic Basic Direct Drive lock provides robust performance with minimal complexity. It supports one Manager code and one 6-digit User code. It features a powerful motorized bolt mechanism that retracts automatically after a valid code is entered. A no‑twist cable design enhances durability, and a single 9‑volt battery powers approximately 10,000 openings. The lock is fully field-programmable and features a standard footprint for seamless retrofitting.

Security Feature:
