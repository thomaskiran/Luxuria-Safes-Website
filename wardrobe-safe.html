Why They Are Called Wardrobe Friendly Safes

Wardrobe Friendly Safes are specifically engineered to integrate effortlessly into standard residential wardrobes without requiring structural modification.

Depth Requirement

Most ready-made wardrobes are built with a standard depth of 55–60 cm. Accounting for door clearance (2–5 cm), the usable internal depth reduces accordingly. To ensure proper fit and usability, all Wardrobe Friendly Safes have a depth of less than 50 cm.

Weight Threshold

Standard wardrobe shelving systems are typically designed to an average load of up to 100 kg. For safety and long-term durability, all Wardrobe Friendly Safes are designed within this weight threshold.

For customers who prefer larger safes with higher capacity, we recommend a custom-built wardrobe solution with a depth of 65–70 cm, allowing for enhanced storage flexibility and accommodation of larger safe models.
